# Official VEC candidate directory — 11 November 2026 release review

## What this change actually delivers

A **searchable public factual directory** of **all 1,037 people nominated** for the 2026 Victorian state election, separate from any seat-probability modelling. It covers **734 Legislative Assembly candidates in all 88 districts**, and **303 Legislative Council candidates across all eight regions**.

The VEC raw workbook was captured from <https://www.vec.vic.gov.au/2026-state-election>, as at **9 November 2026, 7:58 pm AEDT**, with immutable raw source SHA-256 `f5c9505d01abb39bfd4e20cd389c6adea2e524a26423c7c82a51084e21d95c16` established in PR #159. The privacy-minimised CSV `metadata/vec-official-candidates-2026-11-10.review.csv` contains **exactly five approved fields**: chamber, contest, row ordinal, official VEC candidate name and exact registered-party label. CSV SHA-256: `94cabd6ddce086612c8843bcdeb16ad372453de515df19dbcf16fb276b46813b`.

`app/vec-official-candidates-2026.review.json` is a derived static display representation, **not** a forecast input. Node tests prove **all 1,037** displayed rows equal CSV rows, counts reconcile by chamber and 96 contests, ordinals are contiguous, candidate names are unique within contests, candidate contact details are excluded, and source fingerprints agree.

The page provides:

- A dedicated **Official candidates** tab, mobile-friendly expandable electorate/region lists.
- Search by candidate name, district/region or registered-party label, with a chamber selector and source-label party filter.
- All candidates visible within each matching contest, preserving the source spelling.
- Explicit separation from the October 31 experimental model's **five-family assumptions**, which did not use the 2026 ballot as a candidate mask.

## Warnings that must remain visible

1. **VEC registered-party label blank does not mean independent.** There are **91 blank Assembly** and **21 blank Council** registered-party entries. Council unregistered groups and ungrouped nominees require separate ballot-position evidence. The directory reports “No registered party recorded”, nothing stronger.
2. **Source order is not an independently certified ballot order**, especially for Council. A numeric row ordinal from the source workbook is displayed only as *source position*. Do not infer Council group-letter or above-the-line position from the sequential workbook list.
3. **Version dated:** this is a single VEC snapshot of 9 November at 7:58 pm, not a live feed. The VEC may issue corrections; freshness and source-change monitoring must distinguish newer editions.
4. **No automatic officialNomination promotion:** the existing provisional 305-record registry `metadata/candidates-2026.json` remains unchanged. User-facing factual nominations are sourced directly from VEC as a distinct evidence product.
5. **No new model-validity claim:** this change leaves the 31 October experimental forecast, historical scoring, calibration shortfalls, prospective freeze receipts and 6/9 PROD readiness unchanged.
6. **No contact details:** The source XLS also contains contact-person name, email and telephone columns. The public CSV/JSON contain *none* of those fields. Do not add them later without a separately justified privacy purpose.
